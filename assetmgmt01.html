  • Taiwan reshapes its asset-management architecture. Combining a multilateral lender’s origination network with an insurer anchor and an evergreen format creates a scalable access point for pension, insurance and sovereign capital that has struggled to obtain diversified emerging-market private-debt exposure.

Investor tokens are issued on Ethereum and the blockchain constitutes the legal ownership record, rather than merely wrapping an existing fund; the Singapore extension therefore advances tokenisation into regulated active fixed income and cross-border accredited-investor distribution.

UBP reported hedge-fund-only mandates among Asia discretionary clients more than tripling since end-2024; demand concentrates on multi-strategy, macro and quantitative strategies.
