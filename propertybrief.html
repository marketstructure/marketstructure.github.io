Private owners and ex‑owners using this route must now sell their private residential property, in Singapore or overseas, within six months of completing the flat purchase, while the 30‑month wait for subsidised flats and EC units remains. The change follows consecutive quarterly declines in the HDB Resale Price Index in Q1 and Q2 2026.
