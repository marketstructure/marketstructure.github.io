Based on end-2025 data, 76% of Fitch-rated insurers meet the first-stage requirement due at the end of 2026, but 70% fall short of the stricter end-2028 threshold. Minimum equity will be linked to permitted business scope, product range and risk-retention capacity; a risk-based-capital framework scheduled for 2027 could also raise charges. Fitch expects capital injections and consolidation, with life insurers and firms with weaker asset quality or thinner buffers most exposed.
